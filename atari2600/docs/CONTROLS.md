# Pitfall 3: Controls & Gameplay Guide

## 🕹️ Controls

### Joystick
- **Left**: Move character left
- **Right**: Move character right  
- **Up** or **Fire Button**: Jump

### Console Switches
- **Reset**: Restart game
- **Select**: (Future) Cycle difficulty levels
- **Color/B&W**: (Future) Toggle color modes
- **Left Difficulty**: (Future) Player 1 difficulty
- **Right Difficulty**: (Future) Player 2 difficulty

## 🎮 Gameplay Mechanics

### Character Movement
- **Walking**: Hold left/right to move continuously
- **Running**: No separate run button - constant speed
- **Jumping**: Press up or fire to jump
  - Jump height is fixed
  - Can control direction mid-air
  - Cannot jump again until landing
- **Falling**: Gravity applies when airborne

### Physics
- **Gravity**: Constant downward force
- **Terminal Velocity**: Capped fall speed
- **Ground Detection**: Automatic landing on platforms
- **Collision**: Character cannot pass through walls

## 🎯 Game Objectives

### Primary Goal
Navigate through 12 interconnected cavern screens to reach the deepest chamber and defeat the final boss.

### Secondary Goals
- Collect all gold bars for maximum score
- Find hidden artifacts for bonus points
- Discover secret passages
- Achieve the highest score possible

## 💎 Collectibles

### Gold Bars (Yellow)
- **Points**: 100 each
- **Location**: Scattered throughout caverns
- **Effect**: Increases score only

### Artifacts (Cyan)
- **Points**: 500 each
- **Location**: Hidden in secret chambers
- **Effect**: Bonus points + unlock special ending

### Keys (Magenta)
- **Points**: 0
- **Location**: Guarded by enemies or in dangerous areas
- **Effect**: Opens barricaded passages (one-time use)

## ⚠️ Hazards

### Environmental
1. **Pits**
   - Instant death if fallen into
   - Can be jumped over
   - Width varies (1-3 character widths)

2. **Falling Stalactites**
   - Triggered by movement below
   - Fall straight down
   - Lethal on contact
   - Respawn after delay

3. **Rising Lava**
   - Gradually rises from bottom of screen
   - Deadly if touched
   - Forces upward progression
   - Can be temporarily slowed by collecting ice crystals

4. **Rolling Boulders**
   - Triggered by switches or proximity
   - Follow gravity and slopes
   - Can be outrun or jumped over
   - Destroy enemies they hit

### Enemies

1. **Snakes** (Red)
   - **Behavior**: Patrol back and forth on platforms
   - **Speed**: Slow
   - **Pattern**: Predictable horizontal movement
   - **Strategy**: Time jumps or wait for opening

2. **Bats** (Purple)
   - **Behavior**: Fly in swooping patterns
   - **Speed**: Medium
   - **Pattern**: Sine wave vertical movement
   - **Strategy**: Duck under or jump over peak

3. **Spiders** (Green)
   - **Behavior**: Drop from ceiling when player passes
   - **Speed**: Fast (dropping)
   - **Pattern**: Drop, then climb back up
   - **Strategy**: Run through quickly or wait

4. **Scorpions** (Orange) - Later levels
   - **Behavior**: Chase player when in range
   - **Speed**: Medium
   - **Pattern**: Direct pursuit
   - **Strategy**: Use jumps to reach areas they can't

## 👾 Boss Encounters

### Level 4: Snake King
- **Arena**: Large central chamber
- **HP**: 5 hits
- **Phase 1**: Slow slithering, predictable
- **Phase 2**: Speeds up, spits venom projectiles
- **Phase 3**: Erratic movement, frequent spits
- **Strategy**: Hit head when mouth opens

### Level 8: Lava Beast
- **Arena**: Platform above lava lake
- **HP**: 8 hits
- **Phase 1**: Rises from lava, swipes claws
- **Phase 2**: Throws fireballs
- **Phase 3**: Causes lava eruptions
- **Strategy**: Stay on high platforms, attack between attacks

### Level 12: Ancient Guardian (Final Boss)
- **Arena**: The deepest chamber
- **HP**: 12 hits
- **Phase 1**: Stationary, fires in patterns
- **Phase 2**: Teleports, faster projectiles
- **Phase 3**: Multiple forms, screen-filling attacks
- **Strategy**: Learn patterns, use entire arena

## 📊 Scoring

### Points
- Gold Bar: 100
- Artifact: 500
- Enemy Defeated: 50
- Screen Completed: 200
- Boss Defeated: 1000
- Perfect Screen (no hits): 500 bonus
- Time Bonus: 1 point per frame remaining

### Lives
- Start with: 3 lives
- Extra life at: 10,000 points
- Maximum lives: 9

### Game Over
- Triggered when: All lives lost
- High score is displayed
- Option to restart

## 🎵 Audio Cues

### Music
- **Title Screen**: Triumphant fanfare
- **Exploration**: Mysterious ambient theme
- **Danger**: Tense, faster tempo when near hazards
- **Boss Battle**: Epic, intense composition
- **Victory**: Celebratory jingle

### Sound Effects
- **Jump**: Quick ascending tone
- **Land**: Soft thud
- **Collect Gold**: Bright ding
- **Collect Key**: Magical chime
- **Enemy Hit**: Harsh buzz
- **Player Hit**: Descending tone
- **Death**: Long falling tone with reverb
- **Door Open**: Creaking mechanical sound
- **Stalactite Fall**: Whistling descent
- **Lava Bubble**: Popping bass

## 🏆 Advanced Techniques

### Speedrunning
- **Minimum Gold Route**: Skip all optional gold, fastest completion
- **100% Route**: Collect all items, all screens
- **No Death Run**: Complete without losing any lives

### Exploits (Intended)
- **Damage Boost**: Taking damage for invincibility frames to pass enemies
- **Edge Jumps**: Jump from very edge of platform for extra distance
- **Boulder Riding**: Briefly stand on boulder before it hits ground

## 📈 Difficulty Progression

### Screens 1-4: Easy
- Few enemies
- Simple jumps
- Slow hazards
- Long timer

### Screens 5-8: Medium
- More enemies
- Tighter jumps
- Faster hazards
- Moderate timer
- First boss appearance

### Screens 9-12: Hard
- Many enemies
- Precise jumps required
- Very fast hazards
- Short timer
- Boss encounters

## 🔧 Difficulty Settings (Future)

### Novice (B Position)
- 5 starting lives
- Slower enemies
- More time
- Wider platforms

### Expert (A Position)
- 3 starting lives
- Faster enemies
- Less time
- Narrower platforms

## 💡 Tips & Tricks

1. **Learn Enemy Patterns**: Most enemies are predictable
2. **Don't Rush**: Take time to plan jumps
3. **Watch the Lava**: It rises faster than you think
4. **Use Audio Cues**: Sound effects warn of danger
5. **Collect Keys First**: They open shortcuts
6. **Practice Boss Patterns**: Each phase is learnable
7. **Perfect Screens**: No-hit bonuses add up
8. **Remember Layouts**: Screens don't change between runs
